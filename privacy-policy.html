Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who we are

Stick Life Publisher is a personal publishing tool run by the owner of the Stick Life channel. Contact: ozanimation07@gmail.com.

What the tool accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, with their title, description, tags and settings, and to read the owner's channel name and the processing status of the uploaded videos.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or process data about viewers or any other YouTube users.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use YouTube data for advertising or profiling.

What is stored

Only the OAuth access token needed to upload, stored privately on the owner's own computer. No other personal data is stored.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (remove "Stick Life Publisher").

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page.
